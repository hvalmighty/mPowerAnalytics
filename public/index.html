What to upload

  • Portfolio required one row per holding: security code, quantity, and ideally sector. A CASH row for cash.
  • Market prices built into the app (8 Oct 2025 – 8 Oct 2026). Drop a newer PMS price export in box 2 only when you want to update them.

Optional sheets in the portfolio file add more: Transactions and Cash_Flows (exact performance when the portfolio changed), Benchmark_Constituents (sector attribution), Portfolio_Info (dates and VaR settings). Without them the app uses the full price period, treats holdings as unchanged, and compares against the PSEi.
